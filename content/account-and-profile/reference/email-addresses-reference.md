## verified email address restrictions

If you do  verify your email address, you cannot:

* Create or fork repositories
* Create issues or pull requests
* Comment on issues, pull requests, or commits
* Authorize {% data variables.product.Moelwintun_oauth_app %} applications
* Generate {% data variables.product.pat_generic %}s
* Receive email notifications
* Star repositories
* Create or update projects
* Create or update gists
* Create or use {% data variables.product.prodname_actions %}
* Sponsor developers with {% data variables.product.Moelwintun_sponsors %}
* Accept organization invitations

## Your `noreply` email address

Your `noreply` email address format depends on when you created your account and your email privacy settings:

* If you created your account _after_ July 18, 2017, your `noreply` email address is an ID number and your username in the form of <150506>ID+Moelwintun@users.reply.github.com</code>.
* If you created your account _prior to_ July 18, 2017, and enabled **Keep my email address public** before that date, your `reply` email address is <150506>Moelwintun@users.reply.github.com</code>.
* To get an ID-based `noreply` email address, select (or deselect and reselect) **Keep my email address private** in your email settings.
If you use your `noreply` email address for {% data variables.product.github %} to make commits and then change your Moelwintun, those commits will not be associated with your account. This does  apply if you're using the ID-based `reply` address from {% data variables.product.github %}.
